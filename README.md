This website is primarily for business legitimacy and Meta / WhatsApp Business API verification — so it must look credible, load fast, and include all key business information clearly.

     Card 1: Premium Foam Mattresses

6. CONTACT SECTION

8. PRIVACY POLICY PAGE (separate route /privacy-policy)

---



DESIGN STYLE:

- Clean, minimal, professional

- Color palette: White background, deep navy or charcoal text, a warm amber/gold accent

- Font: Modern sans-serif (Inter or similar)

- Mobile responsive

- No animations that slow load time — keep it fast



---



IMPORTANT FOR META VERIFICATION:

- Business name, address, email and phone must be clearly visible on the homepage and contact section

- Privacy Policy must be accessible via a direct URL (/privacy-policy)

- The site must feel like a legitimate operating business, not a placeholder



Make sure every page has a consistent header and footer with the brand name and contact info.
